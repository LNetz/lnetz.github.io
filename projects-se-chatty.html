01SE-Chatty

2025

A teaching assistant that knows this lecture — not just the internet. It answers with the depth of the course material and tells the lecturer where students struggle.

Type
Tool
Built at
RWTH SE
Technique
LLM · RAG
ai.se.rwth-aachen.de/se-chatty ↗

  • The problem

    General-purpose chatbots answer lecture questions with general knowledge: plausible, but often at the wrong level, with different notation than the course, and sometimes simply wrong. Lecturers, meanwhile, only find out what students did not understand when they grade the exam.

  • How it works

    SE-Chatty is fed with the lecture’s own material — scripts, books, slides and videos. For every question it first retrieves the relevant passages and then lets the language model answer on that basis (retrieval-augmented generation). The answers therefore follow the course: its terms, its examples, its depth.

  • For students and lecturers

    • Students ask questions at any time and prepare for the exam with generated questionnaires.
    • Lecturers see which parts of the material are understood — and which aren’t — while the course is still running.
