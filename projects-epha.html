01ePHA

2024 — 2026

Before a doctorate is awarded, the thesis and its reviews are laid out for the faculty’s professors to inspect. ePHA moves this from a room full of paper copies to the web.

  • The problem

    At a German faculty, a dissertation or habilitation is not just accepted by the reviewers: for a fixed period, every professor of the faculty may read the thesis and the reviews and raise concerns before the faculty decides. Traditionally that meant paper copies in the dean’s office — professors had to come by during opening hours to read them.

  • How it works

    The doctoral office creates a display for each thesis: candidate, title, subject group, reviewers and examiners, the documents as PDFs — thesis, summary, bibliography, reviews — and the display period. Professors are notified by e-mail, either immediately or in a weekly digest, filtered by the subject groups they care about. During the period they read everything online and can send a comment — praise, criticism or a neutral note — directly to the dean’s office. Invitations to the defence go out from the same application.

  • Model-driven

    ePHA is a small application by design, and that is exactly where model-driven development pays off: the domain — theses, documents, reviewers, displays, comments, notification settings — is described as a class diagram, and the application is generated from it with the MontiGem tool chain. The result runs in production for the Faculty of Mathematics, Computer Science and Natural Sciences.
