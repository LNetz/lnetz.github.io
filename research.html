02Research

I research how large language models and model-driven engineering can make each other better: LLMs that write valid models, and models that keep LLM output in check.

Topics

LLMs for modeling

Model synthesis in DSLs, grammar masking for syntactically valid output, how LLMs corrupt and repair textual models.

Model-driven engineering

Generators and DSLs that stay maintainable when the model evolves.

Low-code & digital twins

Generating customized low-code platforms and cockpits for digital twins.

Information systems

From model-driven prototype to full-size enterprise application — and retrofitting generation into existing systems.

Community
  • 2026

    MDE Intelligence Co-organizer

    The international Workshop on Artificial Intelligence and Model-Driven Engineering, held with the ACM/IEEE MODELS conference since 2019. It is the forum for both directions of the integration: AI techniques that make modeling and model management smarter, and MDE concepts — DSLs, metamodels, transformations — that make AI systems easier to build and to trust. I co-organized its 8th edition at MODELS 2026 in Málaga, together with Dominik Bork (TU Wien) and Boqi Chen (University of Ottawa).
