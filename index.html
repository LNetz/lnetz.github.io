I am an IT specialist at mgm technology partners, working on ESACA ↗ — a sovereign, on-premises platform for agentic coding in regulated enterprises.

Before that I spent several years at the Software Engineering chair of RWTH Aachen University, where I received my doctorate in 2024 with a thesis on model-driven development of full-size web-based information systems. I led the MaCoCo project, built generator-based systems that are still in production, and researched how LLMs can produce syntactically and semantically valid models.

In 2026 I co-organized MDE Intelligence ↗, the workshop on AI and model-driven engineering at MODELS. Outside of work I enjoy photography and flying my drone.
